Caveats and limitations
-----------------------
1. Model agreement, not physical validation. Both codes use the same cutoff and
   no long-range correction. The check finds coding errors, not physics errors.
2. The flexible bonds and angle make the model SPC-like, not SPC. It is not
   comparable to a standard SPC or TIP3P water result.
3. The `step(ron - r)` switching branch is not exercised: no pair sits exactly
   at the switch-on distance in this configuration.
4. `Reference` is double precision and reaches 1e-14. The CPU and OpenCL
   platforms are single precision in places and reach about 4e-6. Certify with
   `Reference`.
5. Only one fixed configuration is checked (seed 0x5A17E2). A model error that
   is symmetric under this geometry could pass.
