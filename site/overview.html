Nano-CAD

An open, multiscale CAD and simulation platform for atomically precise machines. The worked design study is a respirocyte: a 1 µm oxygen carrier proposed by Robert Freitas, taken from quantum chemistry of its binding site up to its behaviour in the circulation.

Every number on these pages is a simulated estimate from model chemistry and lumped models. Nothing here is a built or tested device, and nothing here is a medical claim.

Where the respirocyte study stands

  • pass A passive heme carrier meets the oxygen flux at 1–3 % by volume with no engine, and beats the active pump on volume and power. ADR-0126
  • limit In the real circulation the tank charges to only a few atm in each loop. The pump design acts as a flow-through pump near ambient, not a 1000 atm store. ADR-0132
  • limit The top pump stage carries back about one O2 per pass, so it pumps with a thin margin (net 0.57), and one pocket of eight stalls. Keep the cascade as margin, not a requirement. ADR-0138
  • pass The site can be self-sustaining with no new material: an iron-repair donor, a 100-fold distal pocket, and a co-located dismutase/catalase catalyst. The ring damage is mediated by H2O2, so the rate is bounded by the measured heme + H2O2 reaction (3.6e3 /M/s). The optimized pocket with a Mn-porphyrin mimic gives a 10-year ring lifetime, so the site outlives the 3.2-year rotor. The ring itself cannot be repaired. ADR-0139, ADR-0140
  • pass The donor and the catalyst are built into the site CAD: a proximal pocket with a donor hydroxyl on its floor (Fe-O 3.61 Å) and a manganese center on the pocket wall (Fe-Mn 6.37 Å). Every carbon stays four-bonded. ADR-0139
  • pass The device's oxygen rotor carries the same repair groups on every one of its eight rim pockets: the catalyst at Fe-Mn 6.2–6.9 Å with an open site, and the donor at Fe-O 7.1–7.6 Å. The arm collar pushes the rotor donor about twice as far as the free site, but both are far inside the 22 Å donor of the repair model, so the distance is not a limit (5.6×107 s−1 at 7.4 Å). ADR-0141, ADR-0142
  • pass An unloaded pocket can show the five-coordinate, domed iron: 0.30 Å out of the N4 plane, with the four Fe–N bonds at 2.02 Å. The four meso tethers do not move, so they take the dome without a rebuild. ADR-0143
  • pass The ring mounts to the diamond wall by a rigid ethynyl link. The corrected build has no five-bonded carbons, and the tethered ring is stiff (tilt rms 0.56° at 310 K) while the free ring is soft. ADR-0137

The full table of tests, methods and uncertainties is in the feasibility summary.

Apache-2.0. Run locally with python3 app/server.py --port 8123. The device explorer and the system model need the server. The atomistic viewer works without it on its saved scene, but needs it to build, optimize or chat. The 3D models and the docs are static.
