Sorting rotor

A disk with twelve pockets on its rim, inside a housing with an inlet and an outlet, and an ejection rod on a prismatic joint. The example reports the simulated geometry and mass next to the figures from Freitas, Nanomedicine 3.4.2. The wall chemistry is modelled in the Selectivity panel below. The model has no solvent.

Selectivity

A binding pocket whose wall carries one functional group. The metric puts each guest molecule in the pocket and moves it through a grid of positions and orientations. The table gives the binding energy as a multiple of the thermal energy kT, for the ethanol and dimethyl-ether isomer pair. The charges and the nonbonded parameters come from stated sources. The model has no solvent and no explicit hydrogen bond, so the ratios are model results, not a prediction of separation in water.
