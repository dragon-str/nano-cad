Respirocyte oxygen chain: feasibility summary

Status: simulated estimates, 2026-09-28. Nothing here is built or validated. Each line names the test, the method, the result and the main uncertainty. The decision records (ADR) in DECISIONS.md hold the details.

The design under test

  1. A rotor with small pockets on its rim takes oxygen from blood.
  2. Each pocket holds one heme-like iron site (Fe-porphine with an axial ligand). The site binds oxygen when the iron is flat.
  3. At the outlet a cam withdraws the axial ligand about 2 A from the iron. The O2 bond weakens by 42-63 kJ/mol, and the oxygen leaves into the tank. Forcing the iron to dome was tested and fails (ADR-0104, 0105).
  4. Each pocket also carries about four water molecules. The tank wall has single-file drain channels lined with hydroxyl groups. The tank pressure pushes the water out through them; the oxygen stays in.
  5. The tank is built gas-filled and never falls below a 10 atm cushion.
  6. A one-electron donor on the housing, on the arc where each site is empty, reduces any oxidized Fe(III) back to Fe(II) as the site passes.

What passes, what fails, what is open

Passes as simulated estimates: selective loading, release by a strain switch, water removal by a drain, idle leak, tank start-up, mean power.

Fails or exceeds: peak power at a 10 s fill is 1.7-2.4x the Freitas maximum. A 20-25 s fill brings it inside. A 20 s fill, or a cam that returns more of its energy, would close it.

Fails: a cam cannot force the iron to dome, by pulling its axial ligand (ADR-0104) or by squeezing the ring (ADR-0105). Withdrawing the axial ligand works instead (ADR-0106).

Not modelled:

  • How the porphine attaches to diamond.
  • Relaxed geometries. Every quantum-chemistry number uses a rigid geometry.
  • Irreversible damage of the porphyrin ring by superoxide and peroxide. This sets the device lifetime.
  • The donor chemistry of the repair station and its potential.
  • Nitrogen build-up in the tank (a small vent is proposed, not tested).
  • Fouling, immune response, manufacturing.

How far to trust this

Every number stacks model chemistry: proxy or idealized sites, UFF walls, rigid parts, two density functionals that disagree on absolute binding by 63 kJ/mol. Each link carries an uncertainty of tens of kJ/mol or a factor of a few. The chain is internally consistent and no link fails outright, so it earns a better model. It does not show that the device works.
